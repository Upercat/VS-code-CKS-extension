# Changelog

## 0.1.3 (preview)

- Reviewed descriptions and usage notes for 31 functions, now also in Spanish, matching the
  [Imperivm Modding Wiki](https://openimperivm.com/). Ten of them had no description before, among them
  `AreaObjs`, `GetClassHealt`, `CheckLsaPath`, `Settlement::SetPlayer` and `Flying::PickFlyingPoint`;
  others such as `ChangeMap`, `GiveNote` and `Ship::ShowBuildAnimation` now describe what they actually do.

## 0.1.2 (preview)

- Revised function descriptions.

## 0.1.1 (preview)

- Credits to David Campos Rodríguez and his [CKS reference](https://david-campos.com/cks/).
- Logo in the README.

## 0.1.0 (preview)

- Syntax highlighting, snippets and Windows-1252 / CRLF defaults for `.vs` scripts.
- Error checking for mistakes the game's compiler rejects, with no errors on the scripts the game
  ships and compiles.
- Completion, hover and signature help, including the type of `this` for a leading `.`.
- AI constants from `DATA/AI/AI.INI`.
- English descriptions for about 1,400 functions; English and Spanish interface.
